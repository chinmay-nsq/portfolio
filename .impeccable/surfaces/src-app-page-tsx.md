Scope: the single-page portfolio at `/` (src/app/page.tsx and its sections). Visitor mode: Experience.

Audience and job: anyone who might hire or work with Chinmay (recruiters, hiring leads, engineering managers, founders, clients), skimming on laptop or phone, deciding whether to reach out. Action: email (primary), LinkedIn/GitHub/phone. Proof: real metrics in src/data/portfolio.ts. Constraints: light theme only, no cards/pages/drawer metaphors, no space or aviation motifs, no loader, first-person human copy with playful moments, nothing invented.

History: the owner rejected the space theme, a library card-catalogue concept (ruled and plain), and a dark look. On 2026-11-04 they asked for "the best one" in a clean light editorial direction, chosen by the agent. Do not reintroduce card/page/catalogue metaphors.

## Direction contract

THESIS: A calm, type-led editorial page where the words and the numbers do the work. It refuses the dev-portfolio default of hero + card grid + skill badges.

OWN-WORLD: White ground, near-black ink with a two-step gray ramp, hairline rules (#e6e7ea) as the only structure, one cobalt accent (#2b45f0) for actions and key numbers. Funnel Display for headlines, Funnel Sans for text. Sections are two columns: title (and one human line) on the left, sticky on desktop; content on the right. No boxes, no shadows, no badges beyond the single "Now" pill.

STORY: The visitor reads one big claim with a real number, scrolls through a statement that lights up word by word, opens the work rows that interest them, sees projects at headline scale, and ends on a large email address.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Name left and section links plus an accent "Email me" pill in the header. The headline "I build software that 5,000+ people use every day." at up to 6rem, 5,000+ in accent, sitting low in the viewport; under a hairline, a short intro paragraph left, Email me and See my work right, and the live "It's 9:42 pm in Pune, so I'm probably…" line.

FORM: Clean editorial, chosen by the agent at the owner's request after the owner declined the dealt and offered directions. Signature interaction: the About statement's words go from faint to full ink as you scroll. seed: none (unavailable)

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance

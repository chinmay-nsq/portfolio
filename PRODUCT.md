# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Anyone who might want to work with Chinmay: recruiters and hiring leads filling full-time software roles, engineering managers checking depth, and founders or clients looking for someone to build a product. Most arrive from a LinkedIn profile, a resume link or a shared URL, skim on a laptop or phone, and decide within a minute whether to keep reading and reach out.

## Product Purpose

A personal portfolio for Chinmay Kalyan Lale, a full-stack software engineer in Pune, India. It shows where Chinmay has worked, what was shipped (with real numbers), side projects, skills and a few wins, and makes it effortless to get in touch. Success: visitors scroll through all of it and email or message Chinmay.

## Positioning

Real production results at a young career stage: a React Native app with 5,000+ daily users, 99.9% uptime on AWS, APIs and caching work with measured speedups, and now GenieHire, an AI recruitment platform at Nsquare Experts. The site itself is proof of craft.

## Content and evidence

All facts live in `src/data/portfolio.ts` (experience, projects, achievements, education, contact). Do not invent metrics, clients, testimonials or links. Project live/repo links are mostly empty; only the SpringCart repo link is set. No portrait photo or CV PDF yet (fields exist to add them).

## Voice

First person, written by Chinmay. Human and specific, sometimes playful or a little funky, never corporate or AI-sounding. No buzzword stacks, no em-dash-heavy prose.

## Brand commitments

- Simple, aesthetic, minimalist, professional.
- The earlier space / fighter-jet theme is dropped (decision 2026-11-04). Do not reintroduce space or aviation motifs.
- Light theme only. No card / page / catalogue metaphors (rejected 2026-11-04).
- No intro loader: name and role must be visible immediately.
- The page should pull visitors to keep scrolling to the end.

## Stack

Existing: Next.js 16 (app router, static export to GitHub Pages), React 19, Tailwind CSS v4, GSAP + Lenis.

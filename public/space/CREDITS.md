# Space footage credits

All clips are cut, re-encoded (H.264, looped with a crossfade) and colour-adjusted only for brightness
from visualizations published by NASA's Scientific Visualization Studio (SVS). NASA media is not
copyrighted and free to use; credit is given as a courtesy.

| File | Source | Credit |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `cliffs-*.mp4`, `cliffs.jpg` | [SVS 14833 – Exploring the Cosmic Cliffs in 3D](https://svs.gsfc.nasa.gov/14833) | NASA, ESA, CSA, STScI. Producer: Greg Bacon & Paul Morris; Director: Frank Summers |
| `nebula-*.mp4`, `nebula.jpg` | [SVS 31356 – Cosmic Caverns in the Cat's Paw Nebula](https://svs.gsfc.nasa.gov/31356) | NASA, STScI. Visualizer: Mark Malanoski; VISTA image: ESO/J. Emerson/VISTA |

Each clip exists as `-1080.mp4` (1920×1080, ~5 MB) and `-4k.mp4` (3840×2160, ~13 MB); the site serves the
4K file only on genuinely high-resolution displays. `*.jpg` are poster frames.
